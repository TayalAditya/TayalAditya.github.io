This Pi

  • Status unknown.

    Pi deploys

    :8099

    Which version each site runs, and the log of every deploy. The status page opens from anywhere.

    open permanent link

    status from anywhere https://tayaladitya.github.io/tp-live/deploy/

The sites live on the institute network, so their own links open from the campus Wi-Fi only; each permanent link forwards to the site's current address. The links marked from anywhere go through a Cloudflare tunnel and open from any network. The tunnel's own address changes whenever the Pi restarts it, so share the link shown here, which always forwards to the current one.

A filled green light means the site answered on the Pi itself; a hollow red one means it did not. The Pi checks every two minutes and reports at least every quarter hour; a change takes up to ten minutes to reach this page.

The Pi also answers to aditya.local where the Wi-Fi carries mDNS.
